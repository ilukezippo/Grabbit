How it works

You keep watching in the apps you already use. When you see something worth keeping, send it to Grabbit.

  1. Share or paste a link

    Tap Share → Grabbit in YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or any app, or copy the link and open Grabbit.

  2. Pick the quality

    Best, 4K, 1080p, 720p… in MP4, MKV or WEBM, or just the audio as MP3, M4A or OPUS. Grabbit remembers what you like.

  3. It's in your Downloads

    Files go to Download/Grabbit, ready in your gallery and music player. Open or share them straight from the app.

Copied a link already? Open Grabbit and it's waiting at the top: one tap to look it up.

Video
YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, X, Reddit, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Snapchat Spotlight, Pinterest, Bilibili and 1,000+ more. To MP4, MKV, WEBM.
Music
SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Mixcloud, YouTube Music, or the sound of any video. To MP3, M4A, OPUS.

YouTube Live, Twitch, TikTok Live, Kick. Saved as MP4 at the quality you pick.
Instagram
Reels, posts, carousels, stories and private posts you follow, at full size.
Photos
X photo posts, direct image links, and the preview picture of any web page.
Your own files
Convert any video or audio on the phone to MP3, M4A, OPUS, MP4, MKV, WEBM or GIF.

Services with DRM (Netflix, Shahid, Disney+, Spotify songs) can't be downloaded. If a site stops working, tap Settings → Update engine: sites change often and yt-dlp keeps up.

Install

Google Play doesn't allow YouTube downloaders, so Grabbit is installed straight from its GitHub releases. It takes a minute.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap Download for Android on your phone.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. Android asks to allow Install unknown apps once: allow it, go back and tap Install.

  3. Open Grabbit

    The first start takes a few seconds while the engine sets itself up. Then share your first link.

If Play Protect says it hasn't seen the app before, tap More details → Install anyway. Grabbit is built from the public source code.

Questions

Is Grabbit free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT License, with no ads and nothing to buy. If it saves you time, you can buy the author a coffee.

Is this a Snaptube alternative?

That's the idea: the same "share a link, pick a quality, download" flow, without ads, pop-ups, accounts or trackers, and with Instagram stories, live recording and a screen recorder on top.

Does Grabbit collect my data?

No. There's no account, no analytics and no Grabbit server. It only connects to the sites of the links you grab, and to GitHub to check for app updates and when you tap Update engine. See the privacy policy.

How do I download Instagram stories?

Go to Settings → Instagram account and sign in once. Then share or paste a story link. Your login stays on your phone and is only sent to Instagram.

Can it record the sound of a live stream or game?

Yes, on Android 10 and newer. Turn on Record app sound in Tools. A few apps block their sound from being recorded; the picture is still recorded.

Where are my files?

In Download/Grabbit on your phone. Videos and photos show up in your gallery, songs in your music player.

A site stopped working. What do I do?

Tap Settings → Update engine. Sites change how they work all the time and the engine is updated to keep up. If it still fails, report it.
